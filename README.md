## Team 1 — Research (human tasks): roles

| Role | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Scanner** | Searches the web for new platforms, job boards, and bounty programs (updated when you ask). |
| **Validator** | Cross-checks each platform against multiple sources; verifies company age, payout method, fees, and red flags. |
| **Analyst** | Categorizes opportunities by skill level and realistic earnings; estimates take-home pay after fees/gas. |
| **Scam Filter** | Applies the red-flag checks below and drops anything suspicious. |

## Red-flag rules (applied to every platform)

- ❌ Asks you to **pay to join/start** → scam.
- ❌ Promises **guaranteed or "passive" income** → scam.
- ❌ No escrow / no dispute system, but handles large payments → high risk.
- ❌ Claims "earn while doing nothing" → does not exist.
- ❌ Payout only in a worthless in-house token with no exchange listing → likely a cash-out trap.
- ✅ Legit platforms: escrow or platform-guaranteed payment, real company history, multiple independent reviews.

## How to use

1. Read `OPPORTUNITIES.md` for the verified list.
2. Pick 1–2 platforms matching your skills.
3. Create the wallet first on your phone (Trust Wallet / MetaMask / Cake Wallet).
4. Start with small tasks to build reputation; scale up.
